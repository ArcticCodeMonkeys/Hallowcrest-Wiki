## Basic Description

A monastic group dedicated to worshiping Dragons and specifically [[Searclaw]]. They have a temple in town and a training grounds at [[Searclaw's Rest]]. Members are typically Dragonborn, Kobolds, Draconic Sorcerers, Ascendant Dragon Monks, or Pact of the Greatwyrm Warlocks. While most members worship dragons for their power, grace, and authority, others simply respect the actions that [[Searclaw]] has taken in the past to protect the plane.  

Ember's Creed believes that dragons should not be feared, and instead they should be welcomed as our protectors. We can live in symbiosis with them, as they protect us we can give them our worship and offerings. We can learn from their ancient wisdom we should strive to be as close to them as possible.
