## History

Aurora College was established in 1679 by [[Aurora Stellaris]] in order increase education in the newly formed [[Hallowcrest]] and to get research partners to continue her study of magic and the stars. Many alumni from the first years have gone on to invent spells, become archmagi of [[The Capital]], or return to [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]] to be professors.
## Basic Description

A large college for bards, wizards, and scholars under the same name (see [[Locations/Hideouts/Aurora College|Aurora College]]). Founded by [[Aurora Stellaris]] and currently run by [[Marcus Stellaris]].

## Renown

Mastering a Spell, Trasmuting, etc.

| Renown | Rewards                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 3      | Enrollment in Aurora College. A player can enroll in Aurora College, but must first score above 75% on the entrance exam. Upon passing, they must spend at least 16 hours per Tenday training to get and retain the Magic Initiate (Wizard) feat. If a player ever misses a week of classes, they lose this benefit for the next Tenday and if they miss two in a row, they lose 2 Renown and are kicked out of Aurora College |
| 7      | Spellcasting Services. You can request spell casting services from select professors at Aurora College. You have 3 Favor, which you can use to request the casting of a spell by expending Favor equal to the spells level. If the spell has costly material components, you must provide them. You regain all Favor at the end of a Tenday.                                                                                   |
| 12     | Well Studied. You become proficient in Arcana and History. If you are already proficient in one of these skills, you instead gain expertise in that skill.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 20     | Blessed by the Stars. You can cast one of the following spells without providing material components or expending a spell slot:<br>- Melf's Minute Meteors<br>- Counterspell<br>- Elminster's Elusion<br>Once a spell is cast by this feature, it cannot be used again until you finish a half rest.                                                                                                                           |
